7. DATASET-AWARE BALANCING STRATEGY
================================================================================
Observation: EyeQ has 10,066 training images (75.5%), while IDRiD has only 329 (2.5%).
Standard random mini-batches would result in IDRiD appearing only once every 40 samples.

Implemented Strategy: Square-Root Temperature Balancing
  Sample Weight w_i for image in dataset d: w_i = 1 / sqrt(N_d)
  Normalized so expected sum of sample weights equals N_train:

  Dataset     Raw Count    Raw Proportion    Per-Sample Weight    Effective Sampling %
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  EyeQ          10,066          75.5%             0.7696                 58.1%
  APTOS          2,934          22.0%             1.4254                 31.4%
  IDRiD            329           2.5%             4.2567                 10.5%
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Outcome: IDRiD representation is boosted from 2.5% to 10.5% (~4.2x effective boost),
           ensuring rural clinical fundus features are actively learned without distortion.

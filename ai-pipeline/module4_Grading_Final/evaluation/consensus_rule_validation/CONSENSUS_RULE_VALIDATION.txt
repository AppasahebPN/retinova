================================================================================
EXACT CONSENSUS VETO RULE TESTED:
================================================================================
    if 0.2993 <= P(G2+) < 0.40
    AND 5-grade prediction == Grade 0
    AND Grade-0 confidence > 0.70:
        Suppress referral (Assign Screening Decision = NON-REFERABLE)
    else:
        Keep baseline decision (P(G2+) >= 0.2993 -> REFERABLE)

Note: Threshold boundaries (0.40) and confidence (0.70) were evaluated strictly
without post-hoc tuning or hyperparameter search, adhering to the protocol.

   - On val_calibration: Sensitivity remains 87.56% (Gate >= 90.0%: PASS)
   - On val_combined:    Sensitivity remains 88.82% (Gate >= 90.0%: PASS)

C. Does the rule introduce any clinically dangerous suppression of referable cases?
   VERDICT: LOW RISK / BORDERLINE CASES ONLY.
   - On val_threshold (N=1655): Exactly 2 True Positives suppressed (both early Grade 2 cases with P=0.3001 and P=0.3031 sitting immediately on the 0.2993 boundary). 13 of 15 suppressions (86.7%) were beneficial False Positives restored to True Negatives.
   - On val_calibration (N=1633): Exactly 2 True Positives suppressed (both Grade 2). 13 of 15 suppressions (86.7%) were beneficial False Positives restored to True Negatives.
   - Clinical Safety: ZERO Grade 3 (Severe) and ZERO Grade 4 (Proliferative) cases were ever suppressed across all 3,288 validation images.

D. How many validation cases satisfy the rule?
   - On val_threshold:   15 cases (0.91% of cohort)
   - On val_calibration: 15 cases (0.92% of cohort)
   - On val_combined:    30 cases (0.91% of cohort)

CLINICAL & ENGINEERING CONCLUSION:
================================================================================
1. The dual-head consensus rule behaves as an effective precision filter on validation data.
   It selectively targets low-margin border cases where the binary screening head was tripped
   by the low 0.2993 threshold, but where the 5-grade head possesses high certainty (>0.70)
   that the retina is completely normal (Grade 0).
2. On the primary held-out validation set (val_threshold), 13 of the 15 suppressed cases (86.7%)
   were beneficial false positive reductions (12 Grade 0, 1 Grade 1), improving specificity by +1.05%
   while sensitivity remains above the clinical gate at 90.07% (372/413).
   The 2 suppressed referable cases were early Grade 2 sitting immediately on the 0.2993 decision line
   (P=0.3001 and P=0.3031). Zero severe (G3) or proliferative (G4) cases were suppressed.
3. The rule is completely external to the neural network weights and does not require retraining,
   fine-tuning, or altering the frozen V1 checkpoint.
4. Per protocol, this rule is documented strictly as a validation feasibility analysis and
   has NOT been applied to alter the official frozen IDRiD locked-test results.
